# Varanasi map data

The bundled Varanasi road network and landmark locations are derived from
© [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
These derived databases are available under the
[Open Data Commons Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

The road extract covers 82.989–83.025° E and 25.292–25.328° N. The routing
data is stored as gzip-compressed JSON. The landmark file contains
longitude and latitude for the locations offered by the incident form.
The vehicles and incidents are simulated.
